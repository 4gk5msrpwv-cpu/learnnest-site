Questions, answered.

What ages is it for?

Best for kids around 6–14. Younger children can use it with a parent's help.

How much does it cost?

Free 7-day trial, then a one-time purchase — no subscription, no ads. The exact price is shown on the App Store. Family Sharing is supported.

Do I need an account?

No. No sign-up, and no data is collected. Everything syncs through your own iCloud.

How many children can I add?

As many as you like — each child gets a separate space.

Which languages are supported?

21, including English, Chinese (Simplified & Traditional), Japanese, Spanish, French, German and Korean.

Is there an Android version?

iOS first for now. Android is being considered.
