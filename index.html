Frequently asked questions

About data, accounts and buying — the honest answers.

Where is my family's data stored? Is it safe?

All data lives only in your own private iCloud database (Apple CloudKit). The developer cannot read or export it. There is no account system, and no third-party analytics or ad SDKs.

Do I need to register an account?

No. Download and start using it right away — sign in to iCloud on the device and sync plus family sharing work automatically.

How does the free trial work?

You get every feature free for 7 days. After the trial, a one-time purchase unlocks everything permanently — no subscription, no hidden in-app purchases.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad, with layouts designed for each size. Data syncs across all your family's devices via iCloud.

Is it available in my language?

Yes — LearnNest speaks 22 languages, including English, Simplified/Traditional Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Spanish, Portuguese (Brazil), French, German, Italian, Russian and more.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Timetables, homework and habit check-ins all work offline; everything syncs through iCloud once you're back online.
